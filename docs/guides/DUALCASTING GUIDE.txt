Doublecasting is the process of casting the Force the Hand of Fate spell twice without using a sugar lump to refill magic. This is done by selling towers after the first cast, which will lower the max magic and thus the magic cost of FtHoF too.
First (convenient) doublecasting spots:
Level 1. 321 => 21 | 3015s
Level 2. 314 => 14 | 3015s
Level 3. 308 => 8 | 3015s
Level 4. 303 => 3 | 2997s
Level 5. 401 => 1 | 3177s
Level 6. 401 => 1 | 2864s
Level 7. 401 => 1 | 3194s
Level 8. 501 => 1 | 2862s
Level 9. 501 => 1 | 3000s
Level 10. 501 => 1 | 3032s(*3017s)
